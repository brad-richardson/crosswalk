Join your city's data to the open map

Look up how your city's own street and path IDs map to Overture Maps GERS ids. Each city gets a bridge table — one file that connects the two ID schemes — so anything keyed to local IDs (crash records, pavement condition, permits, curb rules) can be joined to the open map with one line of SQL.

This is an early work in progress — coverage grows city by city. Spot a problem or want your city added? Feedback is welcome.

Head to Query & browse to explore a dataset or look up a GERS id, or see the worked join example. The stats below are read live from the published index.json.

Methodology notes
What counts as a match. Only rows with match_decision = 'match' drive the headline match rate; review rows ship in the table but are lower-confidence.
Confidence scores. The confidence column is a calibrated probability of a correct match — filter to whatever threshold suits your use case.
Validation varies. Match quality is benchmarked on a subset of datasets; treat un-benchmarked ones as provisional.
